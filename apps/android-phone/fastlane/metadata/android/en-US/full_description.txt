Cablegram turns your phone into the library and remote for Cablegram TV on your Android TV or Google TV.

• Choose videos or folders with Android's file picker. Files stay where they are, and Cablegram only reads what you choose.
• Play them on your TV. On the same Wi-Fi the TV streams straight from your phone over an encrypted connection. Away from home, playback can go through Cablegram's relay, which forwards the video without storing it.
• Control the TV: play, pause, seek, next and volume, from the app or its notification.
• Organise your library: titles, seasons and episodes, and artwork you choose.
• Subtitles: attach .srt or .vtt files and fix their timing. Optional search with your own OpenSubtitles or SubDL account.
• Profiles with their own progress, PINs, and approval before a TV plays a private video.
• Optional: keep videos in your own private Telegram channel.
• Optional: connect to a self-hosted Cablegram Community Server instead of the hosted service.

Cablegram does not provide films, series or channels. It plays videos you already have or are allowed to watch.

Delete your account at any time in Settings, or at api.cablegram.app/delete-account.

Cablegram is an unofficial app. It is not made or endorsed by Telegram. It uses your own Telegram account through Telegram's public API, with Cablegram's own API id.

The phone and TV apps are open source (GPL-3.0): github.com/seep-labs/cablegram-apps
